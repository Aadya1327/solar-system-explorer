Neptune — The Windy Blue Planet

The most distant recognised planet from the Sun.

Overview

Neptune is the eighth planet from the Sun, approximately 4.5 billion km away. It has a diameter of around 49,244 km and several moons.

Surface and Composition

Neptune is an ice giant with no solid surface. It has a deep atmosphere and a faint ring system.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium and methane. Temperatures near its upper atmosphere are approximately -200°C.

Rotation and Orbit

Neptune takes around 16 hours to rotate and approximately 165 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- Neptune has some of the fastest winds in the Solar System.
- Triton, its largest moon, orbits in the opposite direction to Neptune's rotation.
- Sunlight takes around four hours to reach Neptune.
