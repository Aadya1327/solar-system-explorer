Venus — Earth's Twin

Similar to Earth in size, but extremely different in conditions.

Overview

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is approximately 108.2 million km away and has a diameter of 12,104 km. It has no moons.

Surface and Composition

Venus is a rocky planet with mountains, plains and volcanoes. Its surface is hidden beneath thick clouds.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its atmosphere is mainly carbon dioxide, creating an intense greenhouse effect. With an average temperature of around 465°C, Venus is the hottest planet.

Rotation and Orbit

Venus takes 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun and approximately 243 Earth days to rotate once.

Interesting Facts

- Venus rotates in the opposite direction to most planets.
- It is the brightest planet visible from Earth.
- Its atmospheric pressure is about 92 times that of Earth.
