Mercury — The Swiftest Planet

The smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.

Overview

Mercury is the first planet from the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System. It is approximately 57.9 million km away from the Sun and has a diameter of 4,879 km. It has no moons.

Composition

It has a large iron-rich core.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Mercury has an extremely thin exosphere. Its temperature ranges from around −180°C at night to 430°C during the day.

Rotation and Orbit

Mercury takes 88 Earth days to orbit the Sun and approximately 59 Earth days to rotate once.

Interesting Facts

- It is the fastest planet in its orbit around the Sun.
- Despite being closest to the Sun, it is not the hottest planet.
- Water ice exists in permanently shadowed polar craters.
