Moon — Earth's Natural Satellite

Our closest celestial neighbour.

Overview

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It is approximately 384,400 km away from Earth and has a diameter of 3,475 km.

Surface and Composition

Its rocky surface contains craters, mountains and large dark plains called maria. Its gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's.

Atmosphere and Temperature

The Moon has an extremely thin exosphere. Its temperatures can reach 127°C during the day and fall to -173°C at night.

Rotation and Orbit

The Moon takes approximately 27.3 days to rotate and orbit Earth. This is why we generally see the same side of the Moon.

Interesting Facts

- The Moon influences Earth's ocean tides.
- It has different phases, including full moon and new moon.
- Apollo 11 achieved the first human Moon landing in 1969.
