Jupiter — The Giant Planet

The largest planet in our Solar System.

Overview

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun, approximately 778.5 million km away. It has a diameter of about 139,820 km and many natural moons.

Surface and Composition

Jupiter is a gas giant made primarily of hydrogen and helium. It has colourful cloud bands and a faint ring system.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium and other gases. Its cloud tops are approximately -145°C, and powerful storms occur across the planet.

Rotation and Orbit

Jupiter takes about 9 hours and 56 minutes to rotate and 11.86 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- The Great Red Spot is a gigantic storm.
- Ganymede, its moon, is the largest moon in the Solar System.
- Jupiter has a very powerful magnetic field.
