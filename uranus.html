Uranus — The Sideways Planet

An icy giant with an unusual rotation.

Overview

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun, approximately 2.87 billion km away. It has a diameter of about 50,724 km and several natural moons.

Surface and Composition

Uranus is an ice giant with no clearly defined solid surface. Its interior contains water, ammonia and methane-rich materials. It also has faint rings.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium and methane, which gives it a blue-green appearance. Its atmosphere can reach temperatures as low as -224°C.

Rotation and Orbit

Uranus takes approximately 17 hours to rotate and 84 Earth years to orbit the Sun. It rotates almost sideways because of its extreme axial tilt.

Interesting Facts

- Uranus is tilted by approximately 98 degrees.
- It experiences extremely long seasons.
- It is one of the coldest planetary atmospheres in the Solar System.
