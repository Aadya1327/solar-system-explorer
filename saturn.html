Saturn — The Ringed Planet

Famous for its magnificent rings.

Overview

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, approximately 1.43 billion km away. It has a diameter of around 116,460 km and many natural moons.

Surface and Composition

Saturn is a gas giant made mainly of hydrogen and helium. Its spectacular rings consist of ice, rock and dust particles.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its atmosphere contains hydrogen and helium, with traces of methane and ammonia. Its upper cloud layers are around -178°C.

Rotation and Orbit

Saturn takes approximately 10.7 hours to rotate and 29.4 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- Saturn is the least dense planet in the Solar System.
- Titan, its largest moon, has lakes of liquid methane and ethane.
- Its north pole has a remarkable hexagonal-shaped storm.
