Earth — Our Home Planet

The only known planet to support life.

Overview

Earth is the third planet from the Sun, approximately 149.6 million km away. It has a diameter of 12,742 km and one natural satellite, the Moon.

Surface and Composition

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. It has oceans, continents, mountains, forests and diverse ecosystems.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Earth's atmosphere contains mainly nitrogen and oxygen. Its average surface temperature is approximately 15°C, making it suitable for life.

Rotation and Orbit

Earth takes approximately 24 hours to rotate once and 365.25 days to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- Earth is the only known world with life.
- It has a protective magnetic field.
- Its water cycle helps maintain ecosystems.
