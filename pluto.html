Pluto — The Dwarf Planet

A distant icy world beyond Neptune.

Overview

Pluto is a dwarf planet located in the Kuiper Belt. It is approximately 5.9 billion km from the Sun on average and has a diameter of around 2,377 km. It has five known moons.

Surface and Composition

Pluto has an icy surface containing frozen nitrogen, methane and carbon monoxide. Its most famous feature is a large, heart-shaped region called Tombaugh Regio.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Pluto has a very thin atmosphere made mainly of nitrogen. Its average temperature is approximately -230°C.

Rotation and Orbit

Pluto takes approximately 6.4 Earth days to rotate and 248 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- Pluto was discovered in 1930.
- It was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.
- Its largest moon, Charon, is unusually large compared with Pluto itself.
