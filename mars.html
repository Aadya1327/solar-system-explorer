Mars — The Red Planet

A world of ancient rivers and giant volcanoes.

Overview

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, approximately 227.9 million km away. It has a diameter of 6,779 km and two moons: Phobos and Deimos.

Surface and Composition

Mars has a reddish, rocky surface covered with craters, mountains and valleys. Its colour comes from iron-rich minerals in its soil.

Atmosphere and Temperature

Its thin atmosphere consists mainly of carbon dioxide. Its average temperature is approximately -65°C.

Rotation and Orbit

Mars takes about 24 hours and 37 minutes to rotate and 687 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

Interesting Facts

- Mars contains Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System.
- Evidence suggests liquid water once flowed on its surface.
- Scientists are investigating whether life may have existed there.
